# Strategic Foresight & Innovation Researcher

Main portfolio: applied foresight on AI, society, institutions, and human agency, built with
[mechanism-based foresight](https://github.com/wikstrom-bjorn/mechanism-based-foresight) — the
method behind every case here: signal, driver, mechanism, scenario, implication, intervention, in
that order, so scenarios are traceable back to a specific causal claim rather than a pattern-match.

The underlying research and evidence base lives in
[base76-research](https://github.com/wikstrom-bjorn/base76-research); this repository is where that
research gets turned into scenarios, briefings, and design implications.

## Scope

- How frictionless digital systems reshape social integration and agency
- How institutions retain formal legitimacy while losing experiential legitimacy
- How AI alters cognition, authorship, judgment, and epistemic responsibility
- What forms of resistance and orientation remain possible inside adaptive systems
- Translating theoretical diagnosis into design implications for organizations building AI systems

## Cases

- **[Agentic Society 2030](cases/agentic-society-2030/)** — how autonomous AI agents are reshaping
  consumption, institutions, and human agency. Full mechanism-based case: signals → drivers →
  mechanisms → scenarios → implications → opportunities.
