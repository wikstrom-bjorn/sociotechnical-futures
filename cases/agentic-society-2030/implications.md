# Implications

Who is affected by the mechanism in [mechanisms.md](mechanisms.md), and how — concretely, not
abstractly.

## Consumers / individual users

Short-term: measurable convenience gains (time saved, better price discovery — see the 58%-trust and
20%-influenced-orders signals). Medium-term, under scenario A: a diffuse decline in felt control over
one's own consumption and decision-making that is unlikely to be self-diagnosed as agent-related,
consistent with how AoNR documents legitimacy erosion in welfare-state contexts — people rarely name
"friction reduction" as the cause of their own disaffection.

## Platforms and agent providers

Whoever becomes the default "agent of record" for a category of decisions captures durable
transaction-layer value (see [drivers.md](drivers.md)) — but also inherits the accountability question
in [uncertainties.md](uncertainties.md) #5. Under scenario B, providers who cannot demonstrate
"verifiable restraint" face acute reputational and regulatory exposure; under scenario C, providers who
build legibility infrastructure early convert a compliance cost into a trust advantage.

## Regulators and institutions

Article 50 shows regulators currently treating this as a legibility problem, not a delegation-scope
problem (see [signals.md](signals.md)). If [uncertainties.md](uncertainties.md) #3 resolves toward
"stays diffuse," institutions may retain formal legitimacy (compliance, disclosure) while continuing to
lose experiential legitimacy — the exact pattern AoNR predicts, now at the level of individual
market and administrative decisions rather than only welfare-state services.

## Employers and labour markets

The measured wins in agentic deployment (82% of customer-service interactions autonomous, 40% faster
hiring, 55% faster contract review) are concentrated in decision-heavy service and administrative
roles — the same roles most exposed to the friction-reduction logic in [drivers.md](drivers.md). This
implies continued pressure on judgment-adjacent knowledge work, not just routine task automation.

## Designers, researchers, and strategists (the practical "so what" for this portfolio)

The mechanism identified here is not new — it is the same one already documented in a different
domain. That is the actionable insight: organizations do not need to wait for agentic-AI-specific
research to start designing against this failure mode. See [opportunities.md](opportunities.md).
