# Case: Agentic Society 2030

*Status: draft v1, 2026-08-17. Built with [mechanism-based
foresight](https://github.com/wikstrom-bjorn/mechanism-based-foresight). First full demonstration
case for this portfolio — spans AI capability, human behaviour, institutions, consumption, agency,
and governance in a single mechanism-driven analysis.*

## Why this case

Autonomous AI agents — systems that act on a person's behalf across purchasing, scheduling,
correspondence, and decision-making — moved from pilot to visible infrastructure in 2025–2026.
That makes this the case where the full method and the full range of the author's background
(user/service-design practice, sociological theory of friction and legitimacy, and applied AI-systems
work) converge on one question.

## One-line summary

Agentic AI extends the same mechanism analyzed in *The Age of No Resistance* — palliative friction
reduction — from institutional interfaces to individual cognition and decision-making itself; the
open question is whether agency is being outsourced or simply relocated, and who notices the
difference.
